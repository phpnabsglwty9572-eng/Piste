Privacy Policy For Piste
Effective Date: May 18, 2026
1. Introduction
Welcome to Piste ("Piste," "we," "us," or "our").
Piste is a social community application designed for adults who enjoy skiing, snowboarding, outdoor activities, and sharing their experiences.
We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and protect information when you access or use our application and related services (collectively, the "Services").
By using Piste, you acknowledge the data practices described in this Privacy Policy. Where required by applicable law, we will obtain your consent before processing your personal information.
If you have any questions, please contact us at gusttavomacedo@icloud.com.
2. Age Restrictions and Children's Privacy
Piste is intended exclusively for adults aged 18 years or older.
Individuals under the age of 18 are strictly prohibited from registering for an account or using our Services.
We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or process personal information from children under 18.
If we discover that an individual under 18 has created an account or provided personal information, we will take appropriate steps to disable the account and delete the associated personal information, subject to applicable legal requirements and necessary safety obligations.
Parents, guardians, or other individuals who believe that a minor has provided personal information to Piste may contact us at gusttavomacedo@icloud.com.
We maintain a zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse and exploitation, including child sexual abuse material (CSAM).
3. Information We Collect
Depending on how you use Piste, we may collect the following categories of information.
3.1 Account Information
When you register for or use an account, we may collect:
* Email address
* Username and display name
* Profile picture
* Authentication and account identifiers
* Profile information voluntarily provided by you
3.2 User-Generated Content
When you create posts or interact with the community, we may process:
* Photos and videos you choose to upload
* Text, captions, and descriptions
* Audio recordings included in content you submit
* Comments, likes, and other community interactions
* Content reports and feedback submitted through the application
Content you choose to publish may be visible to other users according to the application's available visibility settings.
Please avoid sharing sensitive personal information in public posts.
3.3 Camera Permission
Piste may request access to your device's camera to allow you to capture photos or videos for community posts.
Camera access is used only when you choose to use the relevant functionality.
We do not activate your camera in the background for surveillance or unrelated purposes.
You may deny or revoke camera permission through your device settings.
3.4 Photo Library Permission
Piste may request access to your photo library to allow you to select photos or videos for publication in community posts.
We process media that you choose to provide through the application's upload functionality.
We do not intentionally collect unrelated photos or videos from your personal library.
You may manage or revoke photo library permissions through your device settings.
3.5 Microphone Permission
Piste may request microphone access when you choose to record videos or other supported media containing audio for community posts.
Microphone access is used only in connection with user-initiated recording functionality.
We do not intentionally record private conversations or activate the microphone for unrelated background monitoring.
You may revoke microphone permission through your device settings.
3.6 Device and Usage Information
We may automatically collect limited technical and usage information, including:
* Device type and operating system
* Application version
* Device or application identifiers
* IP address and approximate location inferred from network information
* Application events and feature interactions
* Crash reports and diagnostic information
* Performance and usage statistics
We use this information to operate, secure, maintain, and improve our Services.
3.7 Analytics and Attribution Through Adjust
Piste integrates Adjust, a third-party mobile measurement and analytics service, to help us understand application performance, installation attribution, and user engagement.
Depending on device settings, applicable permissions, and our technical configuration, Adjust may process information such as:
* Application installation and launch events
* Session and engagement events
* Device and application information
* IP address
* Advertising identifiers, where permitted
* Attribution information
* Technical identifiers and performance data
We use Adjust to measure application performance, analyze user engagement, evaluate acquisition campaigns, and improve our Services.
Where required by applicable law, we request consent before accessing advertising identifiers or conducting activities requiring such consent.
We respect applicable device-level privacy choices and restrictions.
For information about Adjust's data practices, please visit:
https://www.adjust.com/terms/privacy-policy/

We may use personal information to:
1. Create and manage user accounts.
2. Provide community posting and interaction features.
3. Process photos, videos, and audio voluntarily submitted by users.
4. Maintain application functionality and performance.
5. Analyze usage patterns and improve the user experience.
6. Detect fraud, spam, abuse, and security threats.
7. Investigate reports of prohibited or harmful content.
8. Enforce our Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
9. Respond to user requests, complaints, and support inquiries.
10. Comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.
We do not use camera, photo library, or microphone permissions for purposes unrelated to the functionality described in this Policy.

Where applicable data protection laws require a legal basis, we process personal information based on one or more of the following:
* Performance of our contractual obligations to provide the Services.
* Your consent, where required.
* Our legitimate interests in maintaining security, preventing abuse, and improving the Services, where permitted by law.

You may withdraw consent where applicable, without affecting the lawfulness of processing conducted before withdrawal.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the reason for processing.
When information is no longer necessary, we will delete or anonymize it using reasonable measures.
Certain records may be retained for longer periods when required for legal compliance, security investigations, prevention of abuse, or the protection of users.
